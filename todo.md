# Illinois Report Card 2026 - EBF updates

### Purposes:
- Provide an update on Evidence-Based Funding adequacy and actual resources using new Illinois Report Card SY26 and EBF Distribution Calcuation FY27 release.
- Highlight [the Peer Illinois Funding tool](peerillinoisfundingtool.org) as an advocacy tool.


### Research questions:
- What is Illinois' adequacy target, actual resources, and adequacy gap (dollars and positions)?
- How many districts are underfunded? How many students are in underfunded districts?
- Where are these districts and students?
- What are the demographics of students by adequacy level?
- How did this change from last year?
- What drove the change? (Resources compared to target, low-income count difference, CPPRT difference)
